# Behavioral questions
---

### **1. Conflict Resolution**  
**Q:** *If you strongly disagree with a teammate’s technical approach (e.g., using Redux vs. Context API), how would you handle it?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Ability to debate constructively.  
- Willingness to collaborate vs. insist on being "right."  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d share my perspective with data (e.g., benchmarks, maintainability trade-offs) and listen to their rationale. If we deadlock, I’d propose a small-scale A/B test or escalate to a tech lead for arbitration."*  

---

### **2. Ownership & Accountability**  
**Q:** *Your merged code caused a production crash. How do you respond?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Accountability and problem-solving under pressure.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"First, I’d roll back the deployment (if possible) to minimize user impact. Then, I’d:  
1. Analyze logs/crash reports to identify the root cause.  
2. Write a hotfix and test it rigorously (unit + integration tests).  
3. Document the failure and share a post-mortem to prevent recurrence."*  

---

### **3. Prioritization**  
**Q:** *How do you decide what to work on when your manager, product team, and tech lead all have competing priorities?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Ability to manage stakeholder expectations.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d align with all parties to understand business impact (e.g., revenue, user experience). For conflicts, I’d propose a priority matrix (urgency vs. impact) and defer to the tech lead’s final call."*  

---

### **4. Mentorship**  
**Q:** *A junior developer is struggling with React Native’s useState hook. How would you help them?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Willingness to mentor and explain concepts clearly.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d pair-program with them to identify misconceptions, share practical examples, and point them to resources (e.g., React docs). I’d also encourage them to debug using console logs and React DevTools."*  

---

### **5. Adaptability**  
**Q:** *The team suddenly needs to migrate from Class Components to Hooks. How do you approach this?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Flexibility and change management.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d:  
1. Create a migration plan (e.g., start with low-risk components).  
2. Document best practices (e.g., rules of Hooks).  
3. Conduct a workshop to upskill the team incrementally."*  

---

### **6. Stakeholder Communication**  
**Q:** *How would you explain a technical delay (e.g., bridging issue) to a non-technical product manager?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Ability to simplify complex topics.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d say: *‘We found an unexpected compatibility issue between our app and Android’s camera API. We need 2 extra days to build a workaround—this ensures the feature works smoothly for all users.’* I’d also provide a trade-off (e.g., temporary feature flag)."*  

---

### **7. Decision-Making**  
**Q:** *Your team debates using an open-source library vs. building a custom solution. How do you contribute?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Critical thinking and risk assessment.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d evaluate:  
- Library maturity (GitHub stars, issue backlog).  
- Maintenance cost (e.g., does it support React Native’s new architecture?).  
- Team bandwidth.  
I’d advocate for the option that balances speed, stability, and long-term maintainability."*  

---

### **8. Handling Feedback**  
**Q:** *A teammate criticizes your code review as “too nitpicky.” How do you respond?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Receptiveness to feedback.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d ask for specifics (e.g., *‘Which comments felt unnecessary?’*) and clarify my intent (e.g., catching edge cases). If my style is disruptive, I’d adapt (e.g., reserve nitpicks for critical paths)."*  

---

### **9. Remote Collaboration**  
**Q:** *How do you stay aligned with a fully remote team across time zones?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Async communication skills.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d:  
- Document decisions in Notion/Slack.  
- Record Loom videos for complex topics.  
- Overlap 2-3 hours with key teammates for real-time syncs.  
- Use GitHub Projects for transparent task tracking."*  

---

### **10. Ethical Dilemma**  
**Q:** *You’re asked to ship a feature with known accessibility issues. What do you do?*  
**What They Assess**:  
- Integrity and advocacy.  

**Strong Answer**:  
*"I’d explain the legal and reputational risks (e.g., WCAG compliance) and propose a timeline to fix accessibility gaps before launch. If pressured, I’d escalate to engineering leadership."*  

---

### **Why These Questions?**  
- **Reveal soft skills**: Communication, teamwork, adaptability.  
- **Simulate real scenarios**: Conflict, pressure, ambiguity.  
- **Test cultural fit**: Alignment with company values (e.g., ownership, mentorship).  

**Pro Tip**: Use the **STAR method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure answers. Example:  
*"When my code caused a crash (Situation), I led the hotfix (Action), reducing downtime by 90% (Result)."*  

These questions help interviewers gauge how you’ll **navigate organizational dynamics** beyond technical skills. 🚀
